# Budget V2 and the genre pass

18 Oct 2026, branch `budget-v2`, merged into main on Maxi's word. This is the record of what
changed, the measurements to compare against later, and the exceptions that are temporary on
purpose. Read it before touching budgets, the box office, the genre market or tentpoles.

## What changed

**Budget V2**
- The size of a picture belongs to the project, never to the player's part (`offers.js
  scaleForOffer`). A lead can be in an indie; a supporting part can be in a feature or a tentpole,
  and then somebody from the world of the right size leads it (`production.js startProduction`).
- Every film project has its own budget (`career/budget.js planBudget`): the scale gives a range,
  the genre places it, production requirements (effects, stunts, period, ensemble, set pieces) and
  the project itself move it. Deterministic per project id, kept once given. Medians for a
  feature: horror ~€33m, drama ~€34m, comedy ~€38m, crime ~€44m, thriller ~€47m, musical ~€52m,
  sci-fi ~€76m; a comedy still runs from under €20m to €90m+. Tentpoles ~€220m.
- The campaign and how wide a feature or indie opens read the real budget (`release.js reachOf`).
- Widths recalibrated on played careers: feature 0.55 → 0.34, indie 0.09 → 0.05.

**The genre pass** (the audit found the genre counted three times)
- APPEAL (`release.js demandCore`) is the one direct genre effect. Untouched.
- The crowd (`audienceFor`) no longer has a genre table below tentpoles, and the run has no cliff:
  a slope from word 52 (×0.78) to 64 (whole run).
- The genre market (`meta/market.js`) judges a genre against its own kind, not against the other
  genres: every release reports `genreResidual` (the game's own arithmetic for an ordinary
  picture of exactly its scale, genre, budget, campaign and part in that market), and a tenth of
  the way back to ordinary every year (`JUDGE`).

## Temporary exceptions — tentpoles keep the old genre physics

Kept on purpose for one pass, to hold the Billion Club where it was calibrated. Not architecture.
When tentpoles are tuned on their own, these go together, and `test_genre_layers.mjs` (the block
marked TEMPORARY) goes with them.

- `release.js TENTPOLE_CROWD` — the old crowd genre table, blockbusters only.
- `market.js m.legacy` — the old market track, judged the old way; `appetiteFor(s, genre,
  'blockbuster')` and `hotGenre(s, 'blockbuster')` read it. Fed by `asOldTrackSawIt`, which tells
  it what each picture would have taken in the old track's heat (without it the track cooled).
- `production.js` — a tentpole's exceptional-shoot roll reads the old track's hot genre.

Measured on the controlled probe, a star's tentpoles past a billion: 17.4% with all of it,
13.5% without the crowd table, 5.3% without the old track, 3.3% without either.

## Known and left open

- Careers' tentpoles past a billion ≈39% → ≈32%. The tentpole arithmetic is unchanged (controlled
  probe). Proven to come from removing the crowd genre table from features (put back for every
  scale, careers return to 37–40%), indirectly through what a player brings to a tentpole. The
  link was not found. Deferred by Maxi until after Media Ecosystem, which changes career paths.
- APPEAL alone still makes the same horror picture take 1.6–1.7× a drama. Separate decision.
- Horror is still the best player genre (66% smash): APPEAL plus the cheapest budgets.
- World sci-fi bombs 42%: the dearest pictures. Look again with the verdict names.
- BROKE EVEN at 0.7× break-even is wrong. Next economic pass: BOMB → LOSS → BROKE EVEN →
  PROFITABLE → SMASH, theatrical language only.

## How to re-measure

```
node tests/probes/probe_verdicts.mjs 40 LABEL                 # careers + world, by scale/rating/genre; run several
node tests/probes/probe_tentpoles.mjs <src/> 10 LABEL         # the Billion Club, controlled; run on two trees
node tests/probes/probe_genre_audit.mjs 12                    # the same film as each genre, traced
node tests/probes/probe_appetite.mjs <src/> 16 LABEL [blockbuster]   # market cycles, either track
node tests/probes/probe_market_par.mjs <src/> ordinary        # ORDINARY should read 1.00 per scale
```
A tree from before is a worktree: `git worktree add <scratch>/wt <commit>`, then pass its
`extracted/src/`; remove it after.
