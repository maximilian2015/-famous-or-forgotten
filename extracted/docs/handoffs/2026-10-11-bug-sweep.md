## Relationships — what is there and why it feels like nothing

There is a lot of machinery: 23 things to do with a person (`life/interactions.js`), dates, moving
in, a wedding with a prenup, divorce (`life/dating.js`), children and adoption
(`life/children.js`), family, a monthly drift that punishes neglect (`life/bonds.js`), nights out
(`social/night.js`, 900 lines), and six kinds of text people send unprompted (`social/sms.js`: saw
your film, went cold, the partner wants you home, a parent saw the papers, proud of a nomination,
drinks).

What is missing is drama. **All seven story chains are about the career** — the papers, the rival,
the agent, the years, the comeback, the one you passed on, the sequel without you. **None is about
a person.** The adult life events are money, burnout, a brand and a mentor. Nearly every
interaction is a button that moves one number and prints a line. A partner never needs anything
from you that is not a dinner; a parent falling ill or dying is a line in the timeline, not a
choice (fly home, or stay on the set); a friend's only request is a drink; and when you are
evicted, only a parent can take you in. People react to your career; they do not have lives that
make demands on it.

That is the gap to design for — not more buttons, but people who want things and act on them.
