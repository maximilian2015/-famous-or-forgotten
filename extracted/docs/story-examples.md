# Story generator — all genres (prototype, branch story-audit)

Not in the game yet. A player would see the synopsis; the twist stays hidden (never on posters, offers or reviews).
Every film below is a real film from Maxi's save, in the order it was made, beside the premise the game gives it today.

- **The checker** writes out every combination, for a hero and a heroine, and looks for: unfilled words, a word twice in a row ("her her career"), he/she with the wrong verb form ("he have", "she remember"), a hero of several people with a singular verb, and the same important word twice in the logline. The first run over the new genres found several hundred problems; all were fixed by rewriting the parts, not by hiding them.
- **Parts say what they need.** A coach party only arrives at a hotel; a dead father's ashes are only driven by his children; a tape "from the first trial" only exists in the sleep clinic; a security officer can erase the footage, a passenger cannot.
- **Memory.** Each film knows the player's earlier stories. In 40 simulated careers of 12 films per genre: a hero repeated in 37–40 careers of 40 without memory, in 0 of 40 with it; the same plot twice in a row 55–80 times without memory, 0 with it.
- **What the checker cannot see** is sense. I read about 120 generated stories by eye and fixed about twenty logic slips of that kind (a phone voice in a story with no phone call, a "partner" for an officer who has none). More will turn up in play.
